Should you ride
today?

Every morning it's the same coin flip at the front door. Bike or Bus pulls live weather for your city, weighs temperature, wind, gusts, and rain - now and in the next few hours - and answers with a single score.

Get my verdict
🚲

One score, 0-100

No decoding weather apps at 7am. 80+ means ride. Under 20 means don't even think about it.

🌏

Any city, live

Type any city on Earth or tap use-my-location. Data comes from Open-Meteo, refreshed every time you ask.

📋

Reasons, not vibes

Every point deduction is itemized - "gusts to 47 km/h: -12". Argue with the math, not the app.

⏰

Next 6 hours

An hourly strip shows what's coming, so a clear morning that rains at noon doesn't fool you.

No sign-up. No tracking. Everything runs in your browser.
